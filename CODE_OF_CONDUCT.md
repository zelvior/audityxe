# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Audityxe is a small, largely solo-maintained project, but contributors and issue reporters are
still expected to treat each other — and the maintainer — with basic respect. This document
sets out what that means in practice.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and any discussion around the project.
- Give and accept constructive criticism about **code and ideas**, not people.
- Assume good faith — most bad bug reports and rough PRs are inexperience, not malice.
- Respect that this is maintained largely by one person (Zelvior) alongside everything else in
  their life — response times aren't guaranteed, and that isn't a reason for hostility.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks directed at the maintainer or other contributors.
- Discriminatory jokes or language relating to race, gender, sexual orientation, religion,
  disability, or any other protected characteristic.
- Publishing others' private information (an email, a real name someone hasn't made public,
  etc.) without consent.
- Sustained disruption of issues, discussions, or pull requests.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies within all of this project's spaces — GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions —
and to any other setting where someone is representing the project or interacting with it in an
official capacity.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to **`zelvior@proton.me`**. All reports will
be reviewed. The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban any
contributor for behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is a condensed, project-specific adaptation in the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), simplified for a small
single-maintainer project rather than copied verbatim from the full Covenant text.
